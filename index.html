About the Data

WeatherGPT never invents a weather value. Every number on this site is retrieved live, at the moment you ask, from named sources below — the conversational layer only explains what those sources returned.

Forecast

Pulled live from Open-Meteo, which aggregates three independent numerical weather models — ECMWF IFS, NOAA GFS, and DWD ICON. WeatherGPT queries all three and shows you where they agree and where they don't, rather than presenting one number as certain.

Warnings

The Warnings page shows live IMD severe weather alerts pulled from the India Meteorological Department's public CAP 1.2 RSS feed. Alerts are polled every 12 minutes, parsed for event type, severity, affected area, and validity window, then served as structured JSON. Below the IMD alerts, a clearly labelled derived risk indicator from Open-Meteo forecast thresholds is also shown for context — never presented as an official alert.

Climate

Historical trends use Open-Meteo's archive endpoint, which is built on the ERA5 reanalysis dataset. Trends compare two equal-length periods and are shown only alongside the underlying averages, never as a bare headline claim.

Confidence

"Confidence" on this site reflects how closely the three forecast models agree for the specific location and time window you asked about — a real, computed spread, not a decorative badge.
